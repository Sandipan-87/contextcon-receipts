Deal board

Every company the agent has scouted or screened, and where it stands. Stage changes are actions the agent executed under the rulebook, or your decisions.

Scout & decide

The agent scouts thesis-fit companies, scores them with what it has learned about you, and seals its predictions before you choose. You pick; it scores itself and learns.

Check a pitch

Paste an inbound pitch. The agent extracts every claim, resolves the company and people on Crustdata, checks each claim within the rulebook's margins, decides, and acts.

What it learned about you

Learned only from your recorded picks and rejects, anchored on the stated thesis. Agreement is scored against predictions sealed before you decided.

Outbox

Emails the agent drafted. They are never sent (handbook rule 4): an analyst reviews and sends them.

Audit trail

Every run is a hash-chained, Ed25519-signed ledger. Verification recomputes everything from the raw API responses; the witness check asks Crustdata's own usage log.

Rulebook

The agent acts on its own only inside these rules (strategy/STRATEGY.md). The full text is sealed into every run, and every action records the rule that allowed it.
